Frequently asked questions

Does registration cost anything?

Registration is free. You enter three things (company email, name and mobile number) and agree to the confidentiality pledge. If we have not been in touch before, we check your registration first and email you.

When can I see prices and documents?

The price of each project (excl. tax), the detailed location with map, documents (with names and account details removed) and Q&A are shown after free registration.

What is the difference between "completed" and "rights transfer"?

Completed: you take over a finished, built plant. Rights transfer: you take over the land and the right to connect to the utility grid (development rights), and the buyer builds the plant.

When is my company name shared?

Not until a meeting is set. Once the date is fixed, ScienceX sets up a Zoom meeting and tells each side the other's company name and attendees.

Who runs Chikudensho Market?
