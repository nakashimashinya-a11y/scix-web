Japan grid-scale battery storage projects for sale: market data

The 283 projects we list, counted by grid area, prefecture, voltage class, structure, status, size and expected grid connection: where the projects are, when they are expected to connect, and how many can be bought as development rights or as completed-build handovers. As of 2026-11-25. The tables are also available as CSV (Japanese).

These are the battery storage projects for sale that have been brought to ScienceX and cleared for listing, all with a grid connection study answer from the utility. They are ScienceX’s own listings, not statistics for the whole Japanese market. Sites and sellers are not published (prices and documents are on Chikudensho Market after free registration). Voltage, structure, status

Voltage class, structure and status

High-voltage projects are mostly around 2 MW; extra-high-voltage projects are roughly 10 MW and above. A project is sold either as development rights that the buyer builds out (transfer of rights) or as a completed-build handover. Status shows whether the utility has answered the grid connection study, whether the grid construction cost has been fixed, or whether the project is already connected.

Download the data, and how to cite it

The tables on this page are also available as CSV (UTF-8 with BOM), rebuilt every morning from the same data. No prices, project names or sites are included.

  • Summary tables (CSV)Grid area, prefecture (top 10), voltage, structure, status, voltage × structure, size, months to connection, planned COD, month first listed

  • Buyer request conditions (CSV)Requests now listed and counts per option
  • Public list (JSON)Public fields per project (grid area, prefecture, voltage, size, status, structure, expected COD; labels in Japanese)
