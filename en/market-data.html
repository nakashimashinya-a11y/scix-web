These are the battery storage projects for sale that have been brought to ScienceX and cleared for listing, all with a grid connection study answer from the utility. They are ScienceX’s own listings, not statistics for the whole Japanese market. Sites and sellers are not published (prices and documents are on Chikudensho Market after free registration). Voltage, structure, status

Voltage class, structure and status

High-voltage projects are mostly around 2 MW; extra-high-voltage projects are roughly 10 MW and above. A project is sold either as development rights that the buyer builds out (transfer of rights) or as a completed-build handover. Status shows whether the utility has answered the grid connection study, whether the grid construction cost has been fixed, or whether the project is already connected.

Grid connection timing

Expected grid connection

Projects with a planned operation date are counted by Japanese fiscal half-year (the fiscal year starts in April); the others by the months to connection stated in the grid connection study answer. 117 projects have a planned date, 116 have no planned date but a stated number of months, and 43 are under review. In all, 150 projects (including some with a planned date) have a stated number of months; the “Months to connection” chart counts these 150.
