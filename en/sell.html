Market Context

Why Battery Storage Land Values
Are Surging Right Now

Grid-scale battery interconnection applications reached approximately 143GW as of June 2025, surging further from ~95GW six months prior. Both extra-high voltage (50MW class) and high voltage (2MW class) projects have exploded. Meanwhile, grid capacity is finite. Favorable connection points are rapidly depleting, and the scarcity of approved projects grows daily.

Whether extra-high or high voltage, the profit structure is the same: projects with lower grid connection costs yield greater profits. Interconnection-approved rights and commercial land are "profit-enhancing assets" for buyers, commanding premium prices.

For extra-high voltage, grid construction costs can range from 2-3 billion yen down to just hundreds of millions for nearby sites. For high voltage, the difference in construction charges — from millions to tens of millions of yen — directly impacts business profitability. This "saved cost" becomes direct business profit, which is why buyers are searching nationwide for favorable projects.

Your interconnection study response or land near substations and distribution transformers holds significant economic value in this market.

Valuation Framework

How Distance from Grid Connection
Dramatically Changes Value

The single biggest factor in battery site value is distance to the grid connection point. For extra-high voltage, this means transmission towers/substations; for high voltage, distribution substations/lines. Closer distance means lower construction costs and higher land value.

Distance from Connection Point Estimated Construction Costs Assessment
Within 300m
HIGHEST RATING 		Extra-high: 200-500M yen
High: Tens of thousands to millions of yen		 Direct connection to tower/pole-mounted transformer possible. Greatest cost advantage for buyers, enabling highest acquisition prices
300m to 1km
CONDITIONAL 		Extra-high: 500M-1.5B yen
High: Millions to tens of millions of yen		 Viable if continuous private line route land can be secured. Key factor is presence of obstacles or third-party land along the route
Over 1km
CASE-BY-CASE 		Extra-high: 2B+ yen
High: Typically not viable		 Extra-high requires new tower construction with significantly higher costs. High voltage generally cannot recover costs at this distance

*The above are general guidelines. Actual construction costs are determined by the grid operator's interconnection study response. Costs vary significantly based on grid configuration, voltage class, and existing equipment conditions. Individual valuations comprehensively evaluate all these factors.
