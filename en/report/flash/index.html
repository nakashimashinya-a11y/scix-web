Three things this month (November 2026)

Three points from the EPRX balancing market and JEPX wholesale figures since November 1, 2026. Charts follow below in the same order as the monthly issue (balancing market first).

  • In the EPRX balancing market, primary reserve (online) had a bid ratio of 131.0% and an average winning price of 2.92 yen/kW per 30 min for Nov 1–25 (preliminary). Last month (October 2026, all days): 145.9% and 3.22 yen.

02

① Balancing market (EPRX) | Primary reserve: bid ratio and price, every day from the 1st

Primary reserve is the fastest product, responding to frequency changes within seconds, and batteries are widely used for it. Online is on the left, offline on the right. Bars show the daily bid ratio (bids ÷ the volume requested by grid operators); gold bars are days at 100% or higher. The line is the daily average winning price.

For Nov 1–25, primary reserve (online) had a bid ratio of 131.0% (last month, all days: 145.9%) and an average winning price of 2.92 yen/kW per 30 min (last month: 3.22 yen). The bid ratio was 100% or higher on 25 of 25 days. Primary reserve (offline): bid ratio 97.0%, average winning price 2.74 yen, 100% or higher on 9 days.

Source: EPRX trading results (preliminary), aggregated by ScienceX.

① Balancing market (EPRX) | Bid ratios by product, every day from the 1st

Bid ratios (bids ÷ requested volume) by product, by day. Above the dashed 100% line, more was bid than requested. Bids can still lose below 100%. The ratio rises when bids grow and also when the requested volume shrinks.

Bid ratios for Nov 1–25: Primary reserve (online) 131.0%, Primary reserve (offline) 97.0%, Secondary reserve 1 152.8%, Secondary reserve 2 295.1%, Tertiary reserve 1 162.4%, Tertiary reserve 2 382.1%, Composite product 151.2%.

09

② Wholesale electricity (JEPX) | Average price, every day from the 1st

Half-hourly JEPX spot prices averaged by day. The dashed line is the system price (Japan); coloured lines are Hokkaido, Tokyo, Kansai and Kyushu.

For Nov 1–25, the system price averaged 28.72 yen/kWh (last month, all days: 22.51 yen). The highest daily average was 37.50 yen on Nov 24 and the lowest 17.20 yen on Nov 1.

Source: JEPX spot market, aggregated by ScienceX.

10

② Wholesale electricity (JEPX) | There is a price gap every day

The daily 4-hour spread: the average of the day's 4 most expensive hours minus the average of its 4 cheapest. The wider the spread, the more room a battery has to earn from it.

Over the 25 days of Nov 1–25, the 4-hour spread was 10 yen or more on 23 days in Hokkaido, 23 days in Tokyo, 25 days in Kansai, 25 days in Kyushu.

Source: JEPX spot market, aggregated by ScienceX.

Average prices for the system price (Japan) and the 9 areas: this month so far (navy) and last month, all days (grey).

12

② Wholesale electricity (JEPX) | How the price moves through the day

Prices for the month so far, averaged by time of day. A battery uses the gap by charging in cheap hours and discharging in expensive ones.

In Tokyo for Nov 1–25, the cheapest half hour starts at 12:00 (23.90 yen/kWh) and the most expensive at 16:30 (40.27 yen/kWh).

Source: JEPX spot market, aggregated by ScienceX.

13

② Wholesale electricity (JEPX) | How large the price gap is per kWh

The 4-hour spread (average of the top 4 hours minus the bottom 4 hours), averaged over the month so far, by area. It ignores the order of cheap and expensive hours, round-trip losses, fees, taxes and network charges, and is not a revenue figure.

For Nov 1–25, among the 9 areas the 4-hour spread was largest in Tohoku (25.58 yen/kWh) and smallest in Tokyo (17.85 yen/kWh).

Source: JEPX spot market, aggregated by ScienceX.
