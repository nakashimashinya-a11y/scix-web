② Wholesale electricity (JEPX): monthly averages and spreads by area

In Oct 2026, the JEPX monthly average for System (nationwide) was 22.51 yen/kWh and the 4-hour spread was 20.42 yen/kWh (source: JEPX spot market).

Wholesale electricity is the electricity that power companies and retailers buy and sell. Its price is set in the "spot market" of the Japan Electric Power Exchange (JEPX) (the market where electricity for the next day is traded the day before). "System (nationwide)" is the price when all of Japan is treated as one market (the system price). When transmission lines between regions get congested, prices split by area from Hokkaido to Kyushu (area prices).

Definitions

Average prices are simple averages of 30-minute slots (not weighted by trading volume). Daytime is the slots from 8:00 to 16:00, and evening is the slots from 16:00 to 20:00.

Spread: we sort the day's 30-minute slots by price and subtract the average of the 8 cheapest slots from the average of the 8 most expensive slots (4 hours) (4 slots for 2 hours). We do not consider the order of cheap and expensive hours, electricity lost in charging and discharging, or fees, taxes and transmission charges. It does not show actual revenue.

③ Capacity market (OCCTO): main auction and the Long-Term Decarbonization Power Source Auction

The capacity market buys electricity supply capacity four years ahead through an auction held once a year.
