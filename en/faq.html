Registration and access

How do I register?

Register for DealRoom, our data room for buyers. Registration is free and the page is in English. Register for DealRoom →

What is the confidentiality undertaking?

It is a short undertaking that you give to ScienceX. The English text is available as a PDF. Sign it and email the PDF to s@scix.co.jp. No original by post is needed.

We review it and then open your access. Access may not be granted in every case.

How soon do you reply?

We reply within 24 hours.

Can I review several projects together?

Yes. You can review several projects as a bundle, including completed high-voltage projects. Each listed project comes with grid-utility correspondence and technical documents, which you request from the project page. A few projects are limited at the seller’s wish.

Prices, language and foreign-investment rules

How are prices shown?

Prices are shown ex-tax, project by project. Where no price is listed, we give it individually.

Can meetings be held in English?

Yes. Meetings can be held in English or Japanese.

Does the foreign-investment prior notification apply to me?

It depends on your holding structure and the project, so we do not answer in general terms. We sort projects against criteria prepared with legal professionals, and refer unclear cases to counsel. Our overview is here.
