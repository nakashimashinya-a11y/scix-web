About Us

Company

Specializing in the "upstream development" of energy infrastructure,
we provide one-stop services from optimal site discovery to rights transfer for grid-scale battery storage.

Mission

Pioneering the Upstream
of Energy Infrastructure.

We complete the most time-intensive and expertise-demanding phase of business development — optimal site discovery and grid interconnection — at our own risk. By providing fully developed projects through rights transfer, we lower the barriers to entry for energy businesses.

Vision

Supporting Sound Investment
Decisions with Neutral Expertise.

From an independent position free of dependency on specific manufacturers or EPC companies, we provide technical evaluation and cost analysis leveraging our Ph.D.-level engineering expertise. We create an environment where businesses can make truly optimal choices.

Message from CEO

CEO Message

Dr. Shinya Nakashima
CEO (Ph.D. in Engineering)
Delivering the right technology
to society in the right way.

Energy is the foundation of society, and its direction shapes the future of people's lives and the environment. That is why we value decisions grounded in engineering evidence, rather than being swayed by marketing claims.

Our work focuses on optimal site development and rights transfer for grid-scale battery storage, as well as thermal energy innovation through black pellets. Both are fields where "technology discernment" determines business success, and where our expertise as a neutral technical PM is most valuable.

Because Science X does not bear construction completion responsibility, we maintain an independent position that allows us to provide technical advice that maximizes your interests — free from the position-taking of specific manufacturers or EPC companies.

Energy is not merely infrastructure — it is the power that shapes the future. Science X will maximize this power and contribute to Japan's carbon neutrality and international competitiveness.

CEODr. Shinya Nakashima (Ph.D. in Engineering)
