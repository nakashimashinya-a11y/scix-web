Privacy Policy

This policy sets out how Science X Inc. (the "Company") handles personal information. It is an English translation of the Company's Japanese policy (個人情報保護方針). If there is any discrepancy, the Japanese version prevails.

The Company recognizes that handling its customers' personal information properly is a social responsibility. It complies with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information and other applicable laws, regulations and guidelines, and handles personal information as set out below.

2. Personal information we collect

When you make an inquiry, ask for a consultation, request documents or apply for any of our services, we may collect the following personal information.

  • Company name, department, job title and name
  • Email address, telephone number and other contact details
  • The content of your inquiry or consultation, the investment size you are considering, and any other information you enter
  • If you sign up for our weekly Knowledge update email: your email address and, optionally, your name
  • Information on your use of our website (access data, cookies and the like; see Section 8)

3. Purposes of use

We use the personal information we collect only for the following purposes.

  • To respond to and contact you about inquiries, consultations and document requests
  • To provide information about, and introduce, our services, projects and funds
  • To notify those who have signed up of new Knowledge articles; you can stop these emails at any time from the link at the end of each one
  • To consider, conclude and perform contracts, and to verify identity
  • To improve our services and their quality, and to analyze how they are used
  • To comply with laws and regulations, and for work incidental to the purposes above

4. Provision to third parties

We do not provide personal information to third parties without your prior consent, except in the following cases.

  • Where required by law
  • Where it is necessary to protect a person's life, body or property and it is difficult to obtain your consent
  • Where cooperation with a national agency or similar body is required and obtaining consent could hinder the performance of its duties

5. Outsourcing

We may outsource all or part of the handling of personal information to the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of use. In such cases, we properly supervise the contractor.

Information you send through our contact form reaches us via infrastructure provided by Cloudflare, Inc., and we use Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.) to send email. We use Blastmail, provided by RAKUS Co., Ltd., to deliver the Knowledge update email, and register the email address and name you give us with that service. We use Google Analytics, provided by Google LLC, to understand how our website is accessed. Any of these may process data on servers outside Japan.

6. Security measures

We take necessary and appropriate measures to prevent the leakage, loss or damage of personal information and otherwise to keep it secure.

7. Requests for disclosure, correction, suspension of use and similar

If you ask us to disclose, correct, add to, delete, stop using, erase or stop providing to third parties your personal information, please contact the contact point in Section 9. After confirming your identity, we will respond appropriately in accordance with the law.

8. Cookies and web analytics

Our website uses an analytics tool that relies on cookies (Google Analytics) to improve our services and understand how the site is used. The information collected this way is anonymous and does not identify individuals. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. For Google Analytics' terms of use and privacy policy, please see Google's website.
