What a switch looks like

Until the new aggregator’s registration goes through, balancing-market revenue may stop. How long depends on whether test results can be carried over. Confirm with both the current and the new aggregator beforehand.

The wholesale and capacity markets are separate procedures. Moving a capacity-market contract needs OCCTO’s prior consent (Capacity Reservation Contract Terms, Article 26).

Only one published example gives a duration

From the decision to switch
~3 months at best
Tensor Energy’s switching support (subject to conditions; announced September 2026)

It covers high-voltage plants of 1 MW or more that are already operating, and only those using EMS from makers Tensor Energy has connected to or plans to connect to. This is one company’s announcement; it does not mean every switch takes three months. That this is the only yardstick is itself where things stand.

Five things to write into the first contract

  1. The term, and the conditions for ending early. How many months’ notice? Is there a termination fee?
  2. Cooperation on exit. The current aggregator helps with registration changes and hands over test results and data.
  3. Who owns the equipment and communications. Whose are the control devices and lines? Can they be used after a change?
  4. When results are reviewed. Results checked every six or twelve months, with a right to end the contract if they fall short of a benchmark.
  5. The name in each market. In whose name the plant is registered or contracted in the balancing, wholesale and capacity markets.

Fees and contract structures are covered in COLUMN 17.

Or split between two from the start

If you own several plants, dividing them lets you compare results without switching. In April 2026 Blue Sky Energy announced this policy as its “multi-aggregator strategy”.

Frequently asked questions

Can the aggregator of a grid-scale battery be changed part-way through?

The change itself is anticipated: EPRX’s FAQ says a transfer procedure is needed for another trading member to use a resource. But the Trading Rules, Trading Guide and process flow contain no transfer procedure, and the documents, duration and cost are not published.

How long does it take to switch aggregator?

Public material does not give a number of days. The performance check is made, as a rule, within three months of receipt of the application under the Trading Rules, and within three months from the day after the performance data are received under the process flow annexed to the Trading Guide. Among private companies there is one example: Tensor Energy announced “about three months at the shortest” for its switching support, subject to conditions.

Does revenue stop during a switch?

In the balancing market, there can be a period when it does. According to EPRX’s FAQ, a bidding unit under examination (what EPRX calls a “list pattern”) cannot be bid. The wholesale and capacity markets are separate procedures.
