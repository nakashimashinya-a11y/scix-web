189 battery, PCS, BMS and EMS products with ★1
— A list with registration numbers, as of 12 November 2026

From IPA's list of certified products (updated 12 November 2026), we extracted the 189 products related to solar and batteries. Each has a registration number, acquisition date, expiry date and headquarters country. The 65 grid-scale products come first. The 10 that expire in May 2027 are shown separately.

A series of four. From April 2027, the control equipment of a battery storage plant must carry the JC-STAR ★1 label to connect to Japan's grid. How to read the tables

  • The registration number is a 16-digit number assigned by IPA. Click it to open the IPA page for that product. The page gives the model number, the maker and the support period.
  • The type is our own grouping. "EMS / site controller" includes PMS and site EMS. "Remote monitoring / gateway / HEMS" includes gateways and HEMS. General-purpose routers (Century Systems, Teltonika, Moxa and so on) are not included.
  • The HQ country is the country of the parent's headquarters. A Japanese subsidiary of a foreign parent is counted under the parent's country. For RENON POWER and LehmanSoft we could not confirm the parent's country, so we mark them "not confirmed".
  • The expiry date is the day before the second anniversary of the acquisition date (the same notation as IPA). Rows that expire by the end of June 2027 are shaded yellow.

Terms used in this series

This series uses the terms below. All four articles share the same table.

Table 0 | Terms used in this series (quick reference)
TermMeaning
JC-STARA security labelling scheme for devices connected to the internet (IoT devices). METI launched it in March 2025. The Information-technology Promotion Agency, Japan (IPA) runs it.
★1 (Level 1)The lowest level. The maker checks the basic rules itself and applies. The rules include no default passwords, a contact point for faults, and software that can be fixed. Third-party assessment applies only at ★3 and ★4.
PCS (power conditioning system)The machine that changes the form of electricity between the battery (DC) and the grid (AC). It is the heart of a storage site.
BMSThe battery management system. It watches the temperature and voltage of the battery.
EMSThe energy management system. It decides when to charge and when to discharge.
Remote monitoring / gatewayThe front door that connects a power plant or storage site to outside networks.
The grid / grid connectionThe grid means the transmission and distribution lines of the power companies. Connecting a power plant or storage site to it is called grid connection.
Grid operator (general transmission and distribution operator)The transmission and distribution companies of the large power utilities. There are 10 across Japan, such as Tokyo Electric Power Grid.
Connection studyA procedure in which the grid operator checks whether a site can connect and how much the works will cost. It comes before the contract application.
Contract applicationThe formal application filed after the connection study reply. It counts as accepted only when the documents are complete and the deposit has been paid.
Extra-high voltage, high voltage, low voltageThe voltage classes of the connection. High voltage is roughly under 2,000 kW and low voltage is under 50 kW. Extra-high voltage is larger than both.

Frequently asked questions

Which makers have PCS with JC-STAR ★1?

On IPA's list of 12 November 2026, there are 14 PCS for storage and grid use. Japan has 7 (Fuji Electric 2, Nissin Electric, TMEIC, Meidensha, Nichicon, Hitachi Energy), Taiwan 2 (TAITEN, Delta Electronics), Korea 2 (Hyosung Heavy Industries, LS ELECTRIC), Europe 1 (Power Electronics) and HQ not confirmed 2 (GBP). No PCS from a China-headquartered maker is listed.

When does a JC-STAR ★1 label expire?

A label is valid for up to 2 years from the date of issue. The 10 labels obtained in May 2025 (GS Yuasa 3, J-City 4, Shizen Connect 2, NextDrive 1) expire between 6 and 27 May 2027. IPA said it would announce the details of the extension procedure "in 2026". As of 15 November, it has not done so.

If a maker is not on IPA's list, has it not applied?

We do not know. The list shows only labels that have been issued. From outside, we cannot tell applications in progress, rejections and non-applications apart. "Not listed" means only that no label had been issued at that time.
